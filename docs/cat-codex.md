# Cat Codex — the scraps of play

Each cat freed in the grey-fight (`game-design.md` §4, "Holding the Grey") is a scrap of a kind of
_play_ the grey could not tidy — too willful, too alive to flatten. The robo-pet, the only one who
remembers across tellings (§2), keeps their stories here; this is what the in-game codex (§6) shows.
Freeing a cat brings its color back to the ship — and reading its page brings back a little of _why_
that color mattered.

> First-draft canon. Names are settled but rename-friendly; memories are written long here and can be
> trimmed for the codex card. The colors each cat restores are listed against the roster in §4.

---

## Scuff — _Force (the Brawler)_ · red & orange, to the hull

A scuffed orange tom with one ear chewed soft. The play the grey can't flatten is the kind that
leaves marks: pillow-fort sieges, broom-handle duels down the hall, the good ache of a skinned knee.
You can tidy a room; you cannot tidy a wrestling match. Scuff brings back the red of that knee and
the orange of the garage worklight — the colors of rough-and-tumble that never asked permission.

## Nona — _Crew (the Matron)_ · hearth-gold, to the common bay

An old calico who counts heads before she sleeps. Her play is the quietest kind — everyone piled on
one couch under one blanket, a hand passing through the popcorn bowl, nobody keeping score. The grey
empties chairs; it has no answer for a pile-up. Nona brings back hearth-gold, the lamp-glow of a full
room — and it aches a little, because she remembers togetherness while the family are still out there
in the dark.

## Glimmer — _Prism_ · spectrum sheen, to the viewports

A sleek cat forever batting at light that isn't there. Hers is the play of wonder: the rainbow a CD
throws on the ceiling, dust turning gold in a sunbeam, a soap bubble held one breath too long. The
grey drains color before it drains anything else — so a cat chasing a refraction is color refusing to
go. Glimmer returns the oil-slick spectrum to the viewports, and starlight comes in colored again.

## Blink — _Hold (the Sentinel)_ · steady teal, to the corridors

A still, watchful cat who stares at the place a thing was. The Hush wins the moment you look away —
forgotten-when-unseen is his whole trick (§2) — so Blink simply doesn't. Her play is the staring
contest and the held breath of hide-and-seek: who can be stillest, who looks first. She pins the grey
down by refusing to forget it. Blink brings a steady, unblinking teal to the corridors — the color of
a held gaze.

## Comet — _Burst_ · flashing accents, to the engines

A kitten asleep until, with no warning, she is everywhere at once. Hers is the play of the sudden
charge — the 3 a.m. zoomies, the dead sprint across the whole house for no reason but joy. You cannot
schedule it, and you certainly cannot tidy it; it arrives like weather. Comet brings the white-hot
flash back to the engines — the streak of a thing moving too fast for the grey to catch.

## Tinker — _Wildcard_ · the color you choose, anywhere

A grey tabby who rearranges the toolbox and knocks things off shelves to see what they do. His play
is making: the blanket-and-chair machine that does nothing, the cardboard-box spaceship, the marble
run built and rebuilt down the stairs. The grey loves a finished, tidy thing; it has no grip on a
thing forever half-built. Tinker doesn't pick your color — he hands you the brush, because invention
is the play of deciding for yourself.
